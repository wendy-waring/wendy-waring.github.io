Welcome About Contact Works News
I was born in Halifax, Canada and grew up within earshot of the steel mills of Hamilton. I’ve lived in Paris, and, through drought on three continents, have nurtured a veggie patch. When I wasn’t translating or editing for feminist publications, I taught in universities around the world.
I’ve had short fiction published in SFF and literary venues, published reviews and essays on fiction and writing in the mainstream and literary press, and translations (French → English) of poetry, fiction and cultural criticism. My BA (Hons) was in French Canadian Studies and Translation (Queens) and my PhD in Comparative Literature (University of Toronto). As a professor of gender and literary studies, I’ve published books, essays and articles. I worked in the literary small press and for sundry academic journals, and as a freelance translator and critic. In the 80s, I worked as a commissioning editor in fiction, poetry and translations for the Women’s Press (Toronto). I was on the collective of the journal Fireweed for that crucial decade when feminism looked up. I still do some developmental work, and embrace literary translation projects that speak to me.
