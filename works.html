The Hunger of Those Who Built It

In this day-after-tomorrow tale of climate apartheid and second-chance redemption set in Paris, two sisters—their relationship tested by a dark family secret—face off as they battle climate catastrophe in very different ways.

Hunger is the story of Diane Griffiths, a sustainability visionary with blinders, who wants to feed the world; Helen, her clandestine sister, who wants to change it; Alain, Diane’s lover, an engineer of history with a Parisian pedigree and a secret Maghreb heritage; and Lou, Diane’s niece and Helen's daughter, a queer rebel and a keeper of hope.
